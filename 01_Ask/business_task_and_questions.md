# 01 – Ask

## Business Context

Cyclistic is a bike-share company operating a fleet of bicycles in Chicago. The company wants to increase the number of annual memberships.

The marketing team believes that converting casual riders into annual members could support long-term business growth. To develop an effective strategy, the company needs to understand how casual riders and annual members use the bike-share service differently.

## Business Task

Analyse 2025 bike-share trip data to identify differences in usage patterns between casual riders and annual members, and recommend practical marketing strategies that could encourage casual riders to purchase annual memberships.

##  Key Stakeholders

- The director of marketing. 
- A team of data analysts.
- The executive team.

## Key Business Questions

- How do annual trip counts compare between casual riders and members?

- How does average ride duration differ between the two rider groups?

- Which months have the highest and lowest trip volumes for each group?

- How does riding behaviour differ by day of the week?

- How does usage vary across the four seasons?

- What patterns could inform strategies to convert casual riders into annual members?

## Scope of Analysis

- Period: January–December 2025.

- Rider categories: Casual riders and annual members.

- Key measures: Trip count and average ride duration.

- Analysis dimensions: Monthly, weekday and seasonal behaviour.

## Expected Outcome

A data-driven analysis and Tableau dashboard that communicate the differences in rider behaviour and provide actionable recommendations to support Cyclistic's membership-growth objective.
